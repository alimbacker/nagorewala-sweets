Our story

Steps from the Dargah, sweet since 1960.

Our first stall still stands on Dargha Market Line at Alangaravasal, Nagore. For more than six decades, families, pilgrims and travellers have stopped by for a box of halwa to carry home.

The recipes have been passed down, not written down. Each batch is cooked in wide pans, stirred by hand until it sets glossy and firm, then loaded with cashews, almonds and dried fruit. It is the same taste people remember, now in Nagore, Chennai and Velankanni.
